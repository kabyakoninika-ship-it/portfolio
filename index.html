01 — About

Economics, grounded in data and the field

I am a graduate of Health Economics with a strong foundation in Economics, Public Health, Statistics and Econometrics. My experience spans research, data management, field coordination and stakeholder communication.

Since September 2026 I have been teaching at Green Ridge International School. Before that, I was a Research and Development Intern at ARCED Foundation, where I supported data cleaning and analysis, enumerator training and field management, and coordinated with partners such as the World Bank and Principal Investigators. Earlier, I was a Research Intern at Palli Karma-Sahayak Foundation (PKSF).

Beyond research, I've led a dance group that performed for the Embassy of the Republic of Korea, edited a university magazine and served on the Health Economics Study Alliance.
